How to Spot a Phishing Message: A Beginner's Guide

Phishing is a type of online scam in which someone pretends to be a trusted person or organisation to trick you into sharing information, opening a harmful attachment or sending money.

Common signs of phishing

  • Unexpected urgency: a message says your account will be closed immediately unless you act.
  • Unusual requests: someone asks for your password, one-time code, bank details or gift cards.
  • Strange links: the displayed name looks familiar, but the actual web address is misspelled or unrelated.
  • Unexpected attachments: a file arrives that you were not expecting, especially if it asks you to enable macros or install software.
  • Generic or unusual language: the message has odd wording, although well-written scams also exist.

How to check a suspicious message

Do not use the link or phone number in the message. Open the organisation's official app or type its known website address yourself. Check account alerts there, or contact support using details published on the official website.

Look carefully at the sender address and full link destination. A padlock or HTTPS connection does not prove that a website is genuine; scam websites can use HTTPS too.

What if you already clicked?

  1. Close the suspicious page and do not enter more information.
  2. If you shared a password, change it on the real service and anywhere else you reused it.
  3. Turn on multi-factor authentication and review recent sign-ins.
  4. If you shared payment information, contact your bank or payment provider promptly using its official channel.
  5. Report the message through the service that received it, and delete it after keeping any evidence you may need.
Remember: Never share a password or one-time verification code with someone who contacts you unexpectedly. Real support teams should not need you to reveal these secrets.

Key takeaway

Pause before responding to unexpected messages. Verify requests through an independent, official channel instead of trusting the links or contact details inside the message.
