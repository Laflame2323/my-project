7 Simple Ways to Make Your Online Accounts Safer

A few consistent security habits can make it harder for someone else to access your email, social media and other online accounts.

1. Use a different password for each account

If one service is breached, a unique password helps prevent attackers from using the same password elsewhere.

2. Make passwords long and difficult to guess

Use long passphrases or randomly generated passwords. Avoid names, birthdays and common phrases.

3. Turn on multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication adds another verification step beyond your password. An authenticator app or security key can provide stronger protection than relying only on a text message.

4. Be careful with unexpected links

Messages that create urgency, promise prizes or ask you to confirm account details may be phishing attempts. Visit the official website or app directly instead of following a suspicious link.

5. Keep your devices and apps updated

Updates often fix security weaknesses. Install updates from your device's official settings or trusted app store.

6. Protect your recovery options

Keep your recovery email and phone number current. Secure your primary email account because it may be used to reset other passwords.

7. Review account activity

Check signed-in devices and recent security activity when a service provides those options. Sign out devices you do not recognise and change your password if you suspect unauthorised access.

Important: Never share a one-time verification code or password with someone who contacts you unexpectedly, even if they claim to be support staff.

Key takeaway

Strong unique passwords, multi-factor authentication, careful link checking and timely updates are simple ways to improve account security.
